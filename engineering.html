ENGINEERING PROJECTS

Thermal Sleeving Fixture

Designed and modeled a tight-tolerance thermal sleeving fixture in SolidWorks to resolve recurring insertion failures; produced GD&T engineering drawings and sourced machined components, eliminating ~$10K/batch rework and reducing insertion failures for TDA Magnetics.

Main assembly shown in large image. Zoomed top view and side view are shown. Top plate drawing shown which was sent to manufactor with full GD&T.

Magnetizing Fixtures

Automated SolidWorks fixture design using equations, design tables, and macros. This allowed magnetizing operators at TDA Magnetics to create new part fixtures with no SW experience, also reducing creation from 15 min to 10 sec when comparing the previous method.

4 samples fixutres are shown in the large gif. Rear view also provided, notice part number and # of fixtures to allow efficent magnetic coil packing. Equations used in the model are shown. Design sheet in excel connected to model is shown.
